When you get in touch

If you send an email, I use your name, email address and message only to reply and to follow up on a possible collaboration. I keep that data no longer than necessary for that purpose.

Statistics

This site uses Google Analytics. If you accept, Google sets cookies and measures which parts of the page are viewed, on which type of device and from which region. If you decline or have not chosen yet, no cookies are set. The page then still sends limited signals without cookies to Google, for example that a page was loaded. These contain no identifier that could recognise you on a later visit. You can change your choice via Cookie preferences at the bottom of the page.

Hosting and transfers

The site runs on GitHub Pages. On every visit GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to serve and secure the page. GitHub and Google may process data in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
